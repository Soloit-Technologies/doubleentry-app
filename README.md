# DoubleEntry

Personal double-entry bookkeeping for Android: accounts, balanced transactions, bank statement
import, recurring transactions, reports, assets and invoices. Your ledger stays on your phone,
with no sign-up, ads or tracking.

This repository is where DoubleEntry gets support. It holds no source code.

## Get help

- **Something isn't working?** [Report a problem](https://github.com/Soloit-Technologies/doubleentry-app/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
  The quickest way is *Settings → Support → Report a problem* in the app, which fills in your app
  and Android version.
- **Question or idea?** [Ask a question or suggest a feature](https://github.com/Soloit-Technologies/doubleentry-app/issues/new/choose).
- Before opening a new issue, [search the existing ones](https://github.com/Soloit-Technologies/doubleentry-app/issues?q=is%3Aissue).

> [!WARNING]
> Issues are public. Never post amounts, account names, backups, bank statements or screenshots
> showing your own financial data. Use made-up examples instead.

## Privacy

Without cloud backup, nothing leaves your device. Optional cloud backup stores your account and ledger in the EU. Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
